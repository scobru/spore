Sovereign Architecture

Built for Crate Diggers & Audiophiles

Designed from the ground up for privacy, longevity, and high-fidelity sound without cloud compromises.

🍄

Pure P2P Crate Digging

Search peer music libraries directly over Freenet without centralized web trackers or corporate servers. Discover rare vinyl rips, underground cassettes, and uncompressed master recordings.

📻

Spore Radio FM

Stream your queue in sequence to listeners worldwide. Spore uses a circular 4-slot ring buffer (~240 KB fixed cap) that overwrites older slots so the network never bloats.

🌱

Radica Companion Bridge

Share spore:radio:<key> links across Radica. Radica monitors your DJ node heartbeats to display real-time 🔴 LIVE NOW interactive cards in timeline feeds and chats.

🏷️

In-Place ID3 & Vorbis Editor

Powered by the Rust lofty engine. Read and write ID3v2, Vorbis Comments, and FLAC metadata instantly on your local files without converting or re-encoding.

⚡

Tauri v2 + Rust Performance

No Electron bloat. Spore compiles to native machine binaries with a tiny memory footprint (~25 MB RAM) and instant startup times across Windows, macOS, and Linux.

🔒

Zero Telemetry & 100% Free

No user tracking, no third-party analytic pings, no accounts, and no walled gardens. Open-source under MIT, peer-reviewed, and fully verifiable.

Decentralized Streaming Protocol

How Spore Streams Without Network Bloat

Traditional streaming uploads infinite GBs to decentralized networks. Spore introduces bounded circular streaming.

🎛️
1. DJ Broadcast Node
Spore desktop app plays local audio queue, slicing 60 KB chunks with Blake3 hashes.
➔
🔄
2. 4-Slot Ring Buffer
Freenet contract state strictly limited to 4 rotating slots (~240 KB total). Old slots are overwritten.
➔
🌱
3. Radica Live Card
Subscribes to 25s DJ heartbeats. Shows 🔴 LIVE status in posts, or marks OFFLINE when DJ stops.
➔
🎧
4. Peer Listeners
Direct P2P audio playback with seamless chunk buffering and zero centralized streaming infrastructure.
